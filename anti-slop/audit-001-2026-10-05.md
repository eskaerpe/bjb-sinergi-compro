## Numbered Findings List

### [01] R-25 — Color Contrast
- **File / Component:** `src/components/sections/Hero.tsx` (lines 196, 207, 218)
- **Rule:** R-25 (Group 1: Hard Gate)
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Reason:** Teks keterangan trust badges (`text-slate-500` ukuran `9px-11px`) di atas background slate tint (`#EEF2F6`) menghasilkan rasio kontras 4.23:1, di bawah standar minimum WCAG AA (4.5:1).

### [02] R-03 — Mobile Responsiveness (Tap Targets)
- **File / Component:** `src/components/sections/Hero.tsx` (line 239) & `src/components/sections/FacilitiesBento.tsx` (lines 231, 239)
- **Rule:** R-03 (Group 1: Hard Gate)
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Reason:** Target sentuh pada tab selector Hero (`min-h-[40px]`) dan tombol navigasi carousel fasilitas (`w-10 h-10` / 40px) berada di bawah standar tap target minimum 44px.

### [03] R-17 — Data & Numbers (Unattributed Metrics)
